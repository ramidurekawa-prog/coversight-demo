# Pre-shift staffing flow: audit findings

Audit of Today, Service and sidebar Data (`/inputs`) on `main` at 0750724
(PRs 78, 80, 82 merged; PR 85 open, touching one line of `service.jsx`).
Baseline: `pnpm typecheck` clean, `pnpm test` 752/752, `pnpm build` passes.

| # | Existing behavior | What it means for the GM | Code entry point | Fix in this pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Today's queue for a room on its own data has no staffing card. The only staffing decision is the sample's written `cut` card. | A GM sees nothing about staffing on Today until someone has already approved a change on Service. | `lib/data/queue.js` `buildOwnQueue`, `lib/data/page/today.js` `todayCards` | One staffing card per upcoming service, built by the shared `staffingCards()` and passed to `todayCards` (`ctx.staffing`) by both browser and server. |
| 2 | Service compares the forecast with the servers *usually* on (history average), never with an actual schedule. There is no schedule input; 7shifts only feeds Today's live tile. | "Send someone home" can be suggested for a person who isn't even scheduled. | `lib/data/page/service.js` `servicePlan` | New `shifts` input (date, role, start, end, optional name, rate, clock-in/out). `staffingPlan()` overlays scheduled servers per hour on the plan; Service's chart and table read it. |
| 3 | A room without Toast is shown the sample's fixed dinner scene on Service, even after the manager enters data by hand. | The manual pilot can't reach a real lunch or dinner forecast. | `service.jsx` (`own = !!fromToast`) | New `services` input (guests and servers by hour, per date). It becomes the room's own service history (`room.serviceOwn`), so Service and Covercast's plan read it instead of the sample. |
| 4 | `POST /api/tasks` with a `cut:` id creates the task without `refuseCut`. | Any page (or a crafted request) can approve a send-home the server would refuse. | `server/tasks.ts` `createTask` | `createTask` re-checks `cut:` ids with `refuseCut` and `shift:` ids with `refuseShift` before inserting. |
| 5 | Service keeps "not tonight / later / hand off" in its own `service.night` key; Today keeps decisions in `queue`. | Two records of one decision; Today never sees Service's "not tonight". | `service.jsx` `record()`, `server/page/today.ts` | The pre-shift decision uses one card id (`staff:<date>:<meal>`) and Today's queue ops (decline, snooze, assign) from both pages, plus one task for an approval. |
| 6 | Tasks store steps and a parse of the approval id, nothing about why. | After new data the original recommendation (forecast, schedule, guardrails, estimate) can't be reconstructed. | `server/db/schema.ts` `tasks` | Two nullable columns, `evidence` (server-built snapshot at approval) and `outcome` (execution and clock evidence). Old tasks keep null. |
| 7 | A task is "verified" only for prices; a staffing change is confirmed by the manager's Done alone. | Manager-reported and clock-confirmed look the same. | `lib/data/writebacks.js`, `server/tasks.ts` `verifyTasks` | `staffingOutcome()` separates decision, marked made, clock-ins, observed straight-time difference, offsets and qualification. A shift task is verified or mismatched from clock-ins once they are entered. |
| 8 | Deep links: `/inputs#kind` only; Service ignores date and meal in the URL and restores one saved view for every room. | A "fix the data" link can't land on the right date and service, and there's no way back. | `lib/pages.js` `pathFor`, `service.jsx` `useView`, `inputs.jsx` | `pathFor` carries `date`, `meal`, `back`; Service honours them over the saved view; Data opens the schedule editor prefilled for that date with a "Back to dinner" link after save. |
| 9 | Data's readiness rows know only the eight existing kinds and their findings, not what Service and Today need. | "Is my data ready for tonight's call?" has no answer. | `lib/data/page/inputs.js` `attention` | `staffingReadiness()` (the same requirement list Service and Today evaluate) shown on Data as "Ready now / Missing / Unlocks". |
| 10 | No role gate on approving a staffing change. | A floor manager can approve a schedule change for the room. | `server/tasks.ts` | A `shift:` approval needs owner, admin or GM. A floor manager can review, record execution and enter data. |
| 11 | Two live-pacing implementations (`lib/data/live.js` for Today, `liveNight` for Service). | Today's tile and Service's call can disagree mid-service. | `lib/data/live.js`, `lib/data/page/service.js` | Not changed in this pass (live same-night calls are out of scope). Noted for follow-up. |
| 12 | `lib/data/queue.js` sizes a finding's minutes as hours × 10. | Shown minutes are 6× too small on findings. | `lib/data/queue.js` | Already overridden by `hoursToMinutes` in `todayCards` for own rooms; left as is. |
